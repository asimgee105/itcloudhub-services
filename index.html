THE QUESTIONS WE HEAR

Let’s make the next decision easier.

Is this a fixed package?+

We begin with your current setup and goals. The scope is then agreed around the work that will make the most difference.

Can you help with one project?+

Yes. A single migration, network refresh or workflow can be a useful place to start.

How do you approach security?+

We look at access, devices, backups and recovery together, then prioritise practical improvements.

What happens after the first conversation?+

We clarify the challenge, identify what needs reviewing and agree on a sensible next step. There is no invented assessment score or automatic quote.
